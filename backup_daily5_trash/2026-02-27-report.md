## 📐 CFA LENS: DURATION & CONVEXITY

**Today's Formula: Modified Duration**

$$D_{mod} = \frac{D_{Mac}}{1 + y}$$

**Where:**
- $D_{Mac}$ = Macaulay Duration
- $y$ = Yield to maturity (per period)

**Worked Example:**
A 10-year Treasury with 4% coupon, 4.03% YTM, and Macaulay duration of 8.2 years:

$$D_{mod} = \frac{8.2}{1.0403} = 7.88 \text{ years}$$

**Interpretation:** For every 100bp rise in yields, bond price falls ~7.88%.

**Current 10Y Application:**
- If yields fall from 4.03% to 3.53% (-50bp)
- Price gain ≈ 7.88 × 0.50 = **+3.94%**

## 🎯 OPTIONS EDUCATION: THE GREEKS — THETA (Θ)

**Theta** = Time decay per day

**Key Insight:** Options are wasting assets. Theta accelerates as expiration approaches (see: "theta burn").

**Example:**
- AAPL $250 call, 30 DTE, trading at $5.00
- Theta = -$0.08/day
- If stock unchanged: Value in 7 days = $5.00 - (7 × $0.08) = $4.44

**The Theta Trap:**
> "Buying weeklies for earnings is gambling with math against you. Theta decay is 3x higher in final week."
> — *Murphy, Technical Analysis of Financial Markets*

**Practical Rule:**
- Sell options when IV is high (collect theta)
- Buy spreads, not naked longs, to offset theta burn

## 📖 GLOSSERY (4 Terms)

**1. Yield Compression**
- *Definition:* When credit spreads narrow, typically during risk-on periods
- *Context:* Current 10Y yield decline despite Fed pause suggests growth concerns
- *History:* Last major compression cycle: 2019 pre-COVID

**2. Theta Burn**
- *Definition:* Accelerated time decay in options' final week before expiration
- *Context:* Retail traders lose ~70% on weekly options (CBOE data)
- *First described:* Black-Scholes (1973)

**3. Flight to Safety**
- *Definition:* Capital rotation from risk assets (equities) to safe havens (bonds, BRK)
- *Today's signal:* BRK +1.5% while AVGO/TSLA down 3-6%
- *Historical parallel:* Q1 2022 Russia-Ukraine invasion

**4. Bearish Flattening**
- *Definition:* Long-term yields falling faster than short-term yields, signaling recession fears
- *Current state:* 10Y at 4.03%, 3M at 3.59% = 44bp curve (was 100bp+ in 2024)
- *CFA relevance:* Yield curve shapes predict business cycles
