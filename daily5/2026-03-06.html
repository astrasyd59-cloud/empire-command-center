🎯 Options Concept: Gamma
2 of 8 — Greek/Concept Rotation

Definition

Gamma measures the rate of change of delta for a $1 change in the underlying stock price. It represents the curvature of the option price relative to the stock price.

Interpretation

Gamma is highest for at-the-money options near expiration. This creates gamma risk — delta changes rapidly, making hedging more difficult as expiration approaches.

▸ Gamma Visualization Key Concept
📚 CFA Level I Formula
Γ = (Change in Delta) / (Change in Stock Price)\n\nGamma is always positive for long options.\nPeak gamma occurs ATM near expiration.

Key Exam Point: Long options = Long Gamma (benefit from large moves). Short options = Short Gamma (hurt by large moves, especially near expiration).

Why This Topic Matters
Corporate Finance (8-12% of exam) covers capital budgeting, cost of capital, and capital structure. Essential for understanding how companies create value.

NPV Rule

Accept projects with positive NPV

WACC

Weighted average cost of capital for discounting

📚 Key Formulas — Corporate Finance
NPV = Σ(CFₜ / (1+r)ᵗ) - Initial Investment WACC = (E/V)×Re + (D/V)×Rd×(1-Tc) Cost of Equity (CAPM): Re = Rf + β(Rm - Rf)

Worked Example: Project costs $1M, generates $300K annually for 4 years, WACC = 10%. NPV = -1M + 300K/1.1 + 300K/1.1² + 300K/1.1³ + 300K/1.1⁴ = -$49K. Reject.

Question 1 of 5 · Corporate Finance
The NPV profile shows NPV on the y-axis and what on the x-axis?
Correct: B. The NPV profile graphs NPV (y-axis) against different discount rates (x-axis). It shows how sensitive the project is to the cost of capital.
Question 2 of 5 · Corporate Finance
When evaluating mutually exclusive projects with different scales, use:
Correct: A. NPV is preferred for mutually exclusive projects as it measures absolute value creation. IRR can favor smaller projects with higher percentage returns but lower absolute value.
Question 3 of 5 · Corporate Finance
According to MM Proposition I without taxes:
Correct: B. Modigliani-Miller Proposition I (no taxes) states that firm value is independent of capital structure. Value is determined by assets, not how they are financed.
Question 4 of 5 · Corporate Finance
The cost of equity under CAPM equals:
Correct: A. CAPM: Ke = Rf + β(Rm - Rf). Cost of equity equals risk-free rate plus beta times the market risk premium.
Question 5 of 5 · Corporate Finance
Operating leverage is highest when:
Correct: B. Operating leverage = Contribution Margin / Operating Income. High fixed costs create high operating leverage — small sales changes create large profit swings.
Today's Glossary — 4 Terms · Corporate Finance
📖 Terms to Know
Net Present Value
The sum of present values of expected future cash flows minus initial investment. Positive NPV projects create value.
CFA Level I · Reading 32: Capital Budgeting
Weighted Average Cost of Capital
The average rate of return required by all of a company's investors. WACC = (E/V)×Re + (D/V)×Rd×(1-Tc).
CFA Level I · Reading 33: Cost of Capital
Operating Leverage
The degree to which a firm uses fixed costs in its operations. High operating leverage amplifies the effect of sales changes on operating income.
CFA Level I · Reading 34: Capital Structure
Dividend Policy
The strategy a company follows in determining when and how much to pay shareholders in dividends. Affects payout ratio and retention rate.
