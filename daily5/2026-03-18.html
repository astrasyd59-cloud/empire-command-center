🎯 Options Concept: Put-Call Parity
6 of 8 — Greek/Concept Rotation

Definition

Put-call parity defines the relationship between put and call prices with the same strike and expiration. Arbitrage exists if this relationship is violated.

Interpretation

Synthetic positions can be created: Stock + Put = Call + Bond (risk-free). This relationship keeps option prices aligned and prevents risk-free profits.

▸ Put-Call Parity Visualization Key Concept
📚 CFA Level I Formula
C + PV(K) = P + S\nWhere: C=Call price, P=Put price, S=Stock price, K=Strike, PV=present value\n\nRearranged: C - P = S - PV(K)

Key Exam Point: Put-call parity applies only to European options on non-dividend stocks. Violations imply arbitrage opportunities that traders quickly eliminate.

Today's CFA Topic: Derivatives
Active Topic
Why This Topic Matters
Derivatives (5-8% of exam) covers options, futures, and swaps. Understanding pricing, hedging, and risk management with derivatives.

Option Payoffs

Max(0, S-K) for calls, Max(0, K-S) for puts

Put-Call Parity

C + PV(K) = P + S for European options

📚 Key Formulas — Derivatives
Call Payoff = max(0, S_T - K) Put Payoff = max(0, K - S_T) Put-Call Parity: C - P = S - PV(K) Forward Price: F = S × (1 + r)ᵀ

Worked Example: Stock at $100, 1-year call strike $100 costs $10. Risk-free rate 5%. Put price from parity: P = C - S + PV(K) = 10 - 100 + 100/1.05 = $5.24.

In a plain vanilla interest rate swap, the party paying fixed:
Correct: B. The fixed-rate payer benefits when floating rates rise above the fixed rate they pay. They receive floating, pay fixed.
Question 2 of 5 · Derivatives
The value of a European call option at expiration is:
Correct: A. Call payoff at expiration = Max(0, S - K). It's worth S - K if in-the-money (S > K), otherwise expires worthless.
Question 3 of 5 · Derivatives
Put-call parity for European options states:
Correct: A. Put-call parity: C + PV(K) = P + S, or C - P = S - PV(K). Violations imply arbitrage opportunities.
Question 4 of 5 · Derivatives
A forward contract's value at initiation is:
Correct: B. Forward contracts are priced so no arbitrage exists at initiation. The forward price is set such that initial value is zero to both parties.
Question 5 of 5 · Derivatives
Delta of a deep in-the-money put option approaches:
Correct: B. Deep ITM put delta approaches -1 (moves $1 for $1 with stock, inverse direction). Deep OTM put delta approaches 0.
